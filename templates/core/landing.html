From a school register to a funded scheme

  1. Schools record who left

    Teachers upload their roll or mark a student as dropped out or transferred, with the reason.

  2. DropWatch finds the groups

    Rates by school, area, gender, social category and class, and the combinations that stand out.

  3. Rules suggest a scheme

    A hotspot's top reasons decide the match: distance points to bicycles, cost to scholarships.

  4. Officers track the result

    Each recommendation is approved, implemented and checked against the next year's rate.
